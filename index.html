Omnichannel Governance Specifications

Technical production rules for Word, Print, Slides, Video, and Web Coding.

Page Geometry & Binding Gutter Standards

  • Formats: A4 (210×297mm) for Learner Guides and PoE Portfolios; A5 (148×210mm) for Laminated Pocket Cards.
  • Margins: Top/Bottom 20mm, Outer 18mm, Inner Gutter 25mm (18mm margin + 7mm ring-binder punch clearance).
  • Bleed: 3mm on full-bleed cover spreads (216×303mm), exported PDF/X-1a at 300 DPI.

Non-Breaking 2-Column Table Rule for Callout Boxes

Never use floating textboxes in Word. Always build callouts as a 1-Row, 2-Column Table with CantSplit = True (Col 1: 22mm icon cell, Col 2: 145mm text cell).

Monochrome Laser Printer Fallback (Canon LBP252dw Compatible)

Emerald Green callouts use a 4.5pt solid black left border + 1.5pt outer border with 5% K tint (#F4F4F4). Steel Blue callouts use a uniform 1.5pt border with 3% K tint (#F8F8F8). Both remain instantly distinguishable in black-and-white.

Classroom Stage Architecture (Option A)

  • Ratio: Widescreen 16:9 (33.867 cm × 19.05 cm / 1920×1080px).
  • Background: Solid Steel Blue #4682B4 with optional organic corner blobs in #D5E3EF and #6DC082.
  • Dominant White Card: Centered 1860×1020px rounded rectangle, 20px radius, 0px drop shadow.

5 Canonical Slide Layouts

1. Title Slide (LB-Logo, 44pt Steel Blue title, 20pt subtitle).
2. Concept Slide (Max 4 bullets, 20pt dark ink, #6DC082 checkmark bullets).
3. 2-Column Contrast Slide (Red mistake left vs. Green audit-ready right).
4. Syndicate Drill Slide (Large 120px symbol on left, drill instructions on right).
5. Statutory Authority Slide (Soft Ice Blue panel with italic legal quote + plain-English translation).

Hyperframes Stage & Motion Grammar (Ratified V8.1)

  • Stage: 1920×1080, Option A persistent white card (30px screen inset, 20px radius).
  • 5px Nesting Rule: Nested panels have 16px radius, 5px outer perimeter gap, 5px flush divider.
  • Caption Bar: Steel Blue background, pure white text, active karaoke word underlined in #6DC082.
  • Transitions: Silent directional panel morphs (top/bottom ±15px, kinetic fires +40px right).
  • Deterministic Blueprint: Strictly typed JSON schema mapping segments to Blocks A, B, C, D, F, or QUOTE.

CSS Tokens & Custom App UI Rules

All cards use background: #FFFFFF; border: 2px solid #83ADCF; border-radius: 16px; box-shadow: none;. Primary buttons use #4682B4 with 10px radius. Action buttons use #1E7E34 (or #6DC082 with #222222 text).

The 7 Core Laws for All AI Models

  1. Clean White Canvas: Long-form content must use #FFFFFF background with #222222 reading ink.
  2. Strict Flatness: box-shadow: none across all mediums. Zero exceptions.
  3. Corner Geometry: 20px containers, 16px callout cards, 8-12px badges and pills.
  4. Single Font: Roboto is the exclusive brand typeface.
  5. Dual-Color Semantics: Steel Blue for structure/law; Emerald Green for action/takeaways; Validation Red for errors only.
  6. WCAG Contrast Law: Never use light emerald #6DC082 for small/medium body text. Use #1E7E34 for green headings and #222222 for reading copy.
  7. South African Voice: Direct, floor-level SA English (Grade 7-8 reading level), coach tone ("you/your").
