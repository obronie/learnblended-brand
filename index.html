Interactive Floor Check • Click an Option to Check Your Answer
A courier arrives at 16:45 with 12 sealed cartons, but 2 cartons have crushed, wet corners. The driver asks you to sign a clean POD now because he is running late. What should you do?
Option A: Sign the POD so the driver can leave, then email the supplier tomorrow morning with photos.
✗ Incorrect — Why This Puts the Store at Risk Signing a clean Proof of Delivery (POD) confirms the stock arrived in good condition. The supplier can reject a damage claim tomorrow because you signed off without noting the wet boxes.
Option B: Write "2 cartons crushed/wet — subject to check" on both copies of the POD and GRV, and get the driver to counter-sign before signing.
✓ Correct — Audit-Ready Floor Practice Recording the exact variance on the delivery note and Goods Received Voucher (GRV) with the driver's counter-signature gives Procurement solid proof for a credit note.
Option C: Accept all 12 cartons quietly and throw the 2 damaged cartons in the skip.
✗ Incorrect — Never Destroy Stock Evidence Throwing away damaged goods causes an unexplained stock shrinkage variance. Always quarantine damaged items in the returns cage and record them on the GRV.

The 10 Core Laws for All AI Models

  1. The Clean White Reading Canvas Law: Long-form learning content MUST use a Pure White (#FFFFFF) background with crisp Dark Reading Ink (#222222) body text. Never place multi-sentence paragraphs inside heavy dark or colored boxes.
  2. The Strict Flatness Law: box-shadow: none across all mediums (HTML, CSS, PowerPoint, Word, Video). Depth is created strictly via borders and soft tints.
  3. The Corner Geometry Law: Outer containers = 20px radius; Callout cards & inner panels = 16px radius; Badges, pills & table wrappers = 8px to 12px radius.
  4. The Single Font Law: Roboto is the exclusive typeface across all digital, video, and print channels.
  5. The Dual-Color Semantic & Instructional Symbol Routing Law:
    • Steel Blue (#4682B4) signals Structure, Authority, Navigation, Statutory Law, Practical Tasks, Incident Analysis, and Assessment. When generating Block 2 Steel Blue Callouts (#4682B4 border, #F4F8FB fill), pair strictly with these 6 Steel Blue symbols: Practical.png, Assessment.png, Information.png, MS-Teams.png, Case-Study.png, or High-Importance.png.
    • Emerald Green (#6DC082 graphics / #1E7E34 text) signals Action, Positive Payoff, Reflection, Self-Evaluation, Multimedia, and LMS Portal tasks. When generating Block 3 Emerald Green Callouts (#6DC082 border, #EAF6ED fill), pair strictly with these 5 Emerald Green symbols: LMS.png, Learner-Guide.png, Discussion.png, Video.png, or Reflect.png.
    • Validation Red (#D9534F) is restricted strictly to Error States, "Wrong Way" contrast blocks, and Quiz Feedback—never decorative.
  6. The WCAG Contrast Law: Never render body text in light Emerald #6DC082 on white. Pair #6DC082 graphics/borders with #1E7E34 for green headings and #222222 for reading body copy.
  7. The South African Floor-Level Voice Law: Direct, honest South African English (UK/SA spelling) at a Grade 7–8 reading level, using a supportive workplace coach tone ("you/your").
  8. The No Legal Disclaimer Law: Never insert legal disclaimers (such as "Training content, not formal legal advice") in videos, BrainCert HTML lessons, PowerPoint slides, or printed manuals.
  9. The Bold-Prefix / Regular-Body Law: Never bold an entire sentence inside interactive options, accordion headers, process steps, or bullet lists. Bold the lead-in prefix in #4682B4 or #1E7E34 and keep the sentence in Regular 400 #222222.
  10. The Visual Illustration & Character Law: All generated graphics must use the Realistic 3D Pixar aesthetic with South African workplace diversity, characters dressed in Emerald Green (#6DC082) collared golf shirts and light blue denim jeans in clean local work environments with Steel Blue (#4682B4) accents.
